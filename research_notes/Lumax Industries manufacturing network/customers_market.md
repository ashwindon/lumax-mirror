# Lumax Industries Limited (LIL): customers, programmes, supply model and market position

Scope note: everything below is about Lumax Industries Limited (BSE 517206, NSE LUMAXIND), the lighting company, unless marked otherwise. Lumax Auto Technologies Limited (LATL, NSE LUMAXTECH) is a separately listed sister company that makes plastics, interiors, gear shifters, CNG parts and runs the group aftermarket business. LATL also shows up as an LIL **customer** in LIL's customer-mix slides. SL Lumax Limited is a 21.28% associate of LIL that supplies Hyundai. Research current to 29 October 2026. The latest disclosures are the Q2/H1 FY27 results released 26 October 2026. FY = April to March (FY27 = April 2026 to March 2027).

Customer-mix figures in LIL decks are "customer-wise sales" of manufactured lamps, **excluding mould (tooling) sales**. The pie-chart values below were read from rendered slide images and checked against the slide totals and the legend colours. Small slices (2-4%) carry some risk of reading error, flagged where it applies.

## 1. Customer list, revenue shares and how the mix changed FY2023 to FY2027

### Takeaway
Five OEMs drive the business: Maruti Suzuki (with Suzuki Motor Gujarat), Mahindra & Mahindra, Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI), Tata Motors and Hero MotoCorp. The top 3 take about 61% of revenue and the top 8 about 85%. Maruti's share fell from 32% (FY23) to 24% (FY25), then recovered to about 26% (H1 FY27) after a ~50% Maruti revenue jump in FY26. Mahindra peaked at about 20% (FY25 to H1 FY26) and is now 16%. Tata (12% in Q2 FY27), LATL, TVS and Toyota are the fastest-growing accounts.

### Inferences
- H1 FY27 vs H1 FY26 growth by customer, calculated from the deck values: Maruti/SMG +33%, M&M +7%, HMSI +11%, Tata +75%, LATL +70%, Hero +15%, TVS about +95%, Toyota +25%. Total manufacturing sales grew +35%. Mahindra is flattening and Tata is surging. (Own calculation from the [Q2 FY27 deck](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/LUMAXIND_26102026201707_LILIPQ2FY2027.pdf).)
- Q1 FY27 can be derived as H1 FY27 minus Q2 FY27: Maruti about INR 306 cr (26%), M&M about 187 (16%), HMSI about 161, Tata about 132, Hero about 76, LATL about 72, TVS about 35, Toyota about 31, SMIPL about 26. A search-result summary of third-party Q1 FY27 coverage gave the same Maruti (INR 306 cr) and M&M (INR 187 cr) figures; that article was not opened to confirm.
- Maruti's share dip from 32% (FY23) to 24% (FY25) is explained by flat Maruti revenue (about INR 675-712 cr) while M&M and HMSI grew. The FY26 recovery came from new Maruti SOPs: e Vitara, Victoris and others.
- For demo modelling, a realistic FY27 annualised customer split (manufacturing revenue about INR 5,000+ cr) would be: Maruti/SMG ~26%, M&M ~16%, HMSI ~13%, Tata ~11-12%, LATL ~7%, Hero ~7%, TVS ~3%, Toyota ~3%, Suzuki Motorcycle ~2%, MG ~1-2%, long tail (Skoda-VW, Honda Cars, Royal Enfield, Yamaha, Bajaj, Ashok Leyland, VECV, Force, tractor makers, Mahindra LMM) ~12%. This is an estimate built from the H1 FY27 slide.
- LATL as a 7% customer: most likely LIL sells lamps to LATL for onward supply. LATL runs the group aftermarket and may also supply some OEM lamp sets, possibly to Bajaj. This is a hypothesis; no source explains the LIL-to-LATL flow.

### Gaps
- No FY24, FY25 or FY26 full-year customer pie was retrieved. The Q4 FY24, Q4 FY25 and Q4 FY26 decks were not obtained (the Q4 FY24 BSE link returned an empty file). FY24 and FY25 Maruti/M&M/HMSI shares come from call statements only.
- No disclosed revenue for Honda Cars, Hyundai, Kia, Skoda-VW, Royal Enfield, Bajaj, Yamaha, Ashok Leyland, VECV, Ather, Ola Electric or tractor makers. All sit in "Others" (about 12%).
- No evidence found of LIL supplying Ather or Ola Electric. Management only refers generically to "active new customer acquisition across EV players".
- CARE and India Ratings rationales for LIL were not found. ICRA appears to be the only rating agency.

## 2. Named vehicle programmes, new programme wins 2024-2026, and EV share

### Takeaway
LIL announces launches and wins every quarter. 2024-26 programmes include the Maruti Swift, e Vitara (full lighting with India's first ADB), Victoris, XL6 and Ertiga; the Mahindra Scorpio, BE 6, XUV 7XO and Thar Refresh; the Tata Tiago/Tigor, Altroz, Punch, Sierra and Aeris; the Toyota Urban Cruiser/Ebella; the Skoda-VW Kushaq and Taigun; the HMSI Activa, Activa EV and Shine; the TVS Apache RTX 300; and the Suzuki e-Access and Burgman. The order book is INR 2,580 cr (Oct 2026): 88% LED, ~11% EV, 63% PV / 37% 2W & 3W.

### Inferences
- The EV share of the order book fell from 37% (May 2025) to 9% (Nov 2025). The likeliest explanation is that the large e Vitara / Toyota Urban Cruiser EV (Ebella) programme moved out of the order book into production (SOP in 2025). This is inference; the company did not explain the drop.
- At INR 400-500 cr a year, the e Vitara alone would be roughly 8-10% of LIL's FY27 revenue run-rate, making it the single biggest EV programme. It is built for export volumes at Suzuki Motor Gujarat. (Inference from the call figure and the H1 FY27 run-rate.)
- Demo-ready programme list per customer, from the sources above: Maruti/SMG (Swift, e Vitara, Victoris, Grand Vitara spot lamps, XL6/Ertiga, legacy hatchbacks and sedans); M&M (Scorpio, Thar 5-door / Thar Refresh, BE 6, XUV 7XO, Veero, UDO 3W, Jawa 2W); Tata (Tiago, Tigor, Altroz, Punch, Sierra, Aeris); Toyota (Urban Cruiser, Urban Cruiser Ebella); Skoda-VW (Kushaq, Taigun); HMSI (Activa, Activa EV, Shine/CD110); TVS (Apache RTX 300); Suzuki 2W (e-Access, Burgman Street); CV (VECV Titan, Force Traveller 2).
