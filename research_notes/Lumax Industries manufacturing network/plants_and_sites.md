# Lumax Industries Limited (LIL): manufacturing plants, R&D and design centres, and other sites (as of October 2026)

Scope note: this file covers Lumax Industries Limited (NSE: LUMAXIND, automotive lighting) only. Sister company Lumax Auto Technologies Limited (LATL) and the associate SL Lumax Limited are mentioned only where needed to explain shared towns or group-level counts, and they are labelled. Source documents used most: the FY2025-26 Integrated Annual Report (published mid-2026), the FY2024-25, FY2023-24, FY2022-23, FY2021-22 and FY2020-21 annual reports, the Q2 & H1 FY27 investor presentation, earnings-call transcripts up to Q1 FY27 (11 Aug 2026), the ICRA rating rationale of 27 Apr 2026, and plant certificates on the company website.

Coordinate method: no source gives plot-level latitude/longitude. Coordinates below come from OpenStreetMap Nominatim lookups of the named industrial estate, village or locality, unless marked otherwise. Confidence: **A** = OSM matched the exact street address; **B** = OSM matched the named industrial estate or village, so the plot is probably within about 1–2 km; **C** = only the town or a nearby landmark matched, about 2–5 km, or my own estimate. Treat B and C as placeholders for a demo map, not survey-grade positions.

## Q1. Current Lumax Industries manufacturing plants: addresses, coordinates, start year, products, customers, capacity

### Takeaway
On 31 March 2026 LIL had **11 manufacturing locations of its own**, all in India. They are 3 in Haryana (Bawal lighting, Bawal SMT electronics, Dharuhera), 2 in Uttarakhand (Pantnagar, Haridwar), 3 in Maharashtra (Chakan II at Mahalunge Ingle, Chakan III at Sawardari, Chinchwad), 2 in Gujarat (Sanand D-1, Sanand E-1) and 1 in Karnataka (Bidadi, Bengaluru). The company's headline figure of "12 manufacturing facilities" also counts the associate SL Lumax's plant at Sriperumbudur in Tamil Nadu. A second Bengaluru plant (greenfield, Bidadi) is under construction, with commissioning expected in Q4 FY27 (January–March 2027).

### Inferences
- **Chakan naming.** The FY24 map shows "Chakan" and "Chakan II". The FY25 and FY26 maps show "Chakan II" and "Chakan III", and there were exactly two Chakan addresses in both years. So Chakan II is almost certainly the older Mahalunge Ingle plant (608-609 Chakan-Talegaon Road, set up in 2005 and expanded in 2008), and Chakan III is the Sawardari / Horizon Industrial Park greenfield plant (1 Nov 2023). The Q1 FY27 call confirms that the Skoda/VW (Phase 2) business "started from our Chakan 3 facility", which fits this reading.
- **Sanand naming.** The plants were renumbered: original Plant 1 = D-1 (FY18). Plant 2 = D-2, Chharodi (commercial production 29 Mar 2022, closed and merged in Feb 2025). Plant 3 = E-1 (approved in Mar 2024). The FY26 map labels E-1 as "Sanand II", so today "Sanand" = D-1 and "Sanand II" = E-1.
- **Likely OEM clusters, inferred from location and not stated plant by plant.** Pantnagar sits in the SIDCUL Pantnagar cluster: Tata Motors (Ace/Intra), Bajaj Auto, Ashok Leyland and TVS-area suppliers. An aggregator says the Pantnagar greenfield was "for supplies of automotive lighting to Tata Motors" ([Goodreturns history, not opened](https://www.goodreturns.in/company/lumax-industries/history.html)); this is unverified. Dharuhera is close to Hero MotoCorp Dharuhera, Honda 2W at Manesar/Tapukara and Maruti Manesar. Bawal is about 45–60 km from Maruti Manesar/Gurugram and near Honda Cars Tapukara. Chinchwad is close to Tata Motors Pimpri and Bajaj Akurdi. Chakan II is close to Mahindra Chakan, VW Chakan and Bajaj Chakan.
- **Coordinates.** Grades B and C are not plot-level. For a demo, Chakan III and the Bidadi plant are the most reliable. Bawal, Pantnagar and Haridwar are the least reliable and should be pinned manually on satellite imagery if precision matters.

## Q2. Recent and under-construction plants (2023–2026), and closed or relocated sites

### Takeaway
From 2023 to 2026 the network changed in five ways. The Gurugram plant closed (May 2023, moved to Dharuhera). The Chakan III greenfield opened (Nov 2023, Phase 2 in Q4 FY26). Sanand D-2 merged into a new E-1 plant (Feb 2025). A new greenfield plant at Bidadi, Bengaluru (₹140 crore) is under construction for Maruti Suzuki and Toyota, with commissioning expected in Q4 FY27.
