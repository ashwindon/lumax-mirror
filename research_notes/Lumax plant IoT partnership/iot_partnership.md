# Lumax plant IoT / real-time machine-data partnership (2024 to 29 Oct 2026)

Scope and method: I looked for a named technology partner that collects real-time IoT or machine data from Lumax plants, covering **Lumax Industries Ltd (LIL)**, **Lumax Auto Technologies Ltd (LATL)** and the wider **Lumax World / DK Jain group**. Sources checked:
- Web and news searches (Autocar Professional, ET-style outlets, vendor pages, LinkedIn-indexed pages, job boards).
- Vendor-name searches: Altizon/Datonis, Infinite Uptime, Faclon, Ripik, Litmus, Siemens, Rockwell, Bosch, PTC, Tata Technologies, HCLTech, Wipro, Tech Mahindra, Accenture, Airtel, Jio, Tata Communications, Vodafone Idea, and Indian IoT energy-monitoring startups.
- Full-text grep of the primary filings: LIL Integrated AR FY2025-26; LATL Integrated AR FY2025-26 and FY2024-25; LATL Q4 FY26 investor presentation; LIL Q1 FY27 earnings-call transcript (11 Aug 2026).

I did not repeat findings already in `Lumax Industries manufacturing network/supply_chain_it.md` (SAP S/4HANA RISE, LSETU, EDI, Bawal MES, 3DEXPERIENCE, the CoE job ad basics, CDIO appointment), except where I re-checked them.

**Bottom line: I could not find any public announcement, press release, vendor case study, customer-logo listing, award entry or filing that names a technology company collecting real-time IoT or machine data from Lumax plants.** The facts below are what *is* documented, by entity, and the closest candidates.

## 1. Which technology company did Lumax work with for IIoT / machine connectivity / OEE / energy / MES / command centre?

### Takeaway
No partner could be identified. The filings describe the capabilities without naming any vendor or system integrator:
- LIL: an MES at its Bawal SMT electronics plant.
- LATL: "IoT-enabled controls" monitoring energy "at a granular, machine-specific level", and "centralized data management systems across 30 manufacturing and engineering facilities" with real-time KPI dashboards.
